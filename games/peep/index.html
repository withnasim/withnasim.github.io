Peep – My Little Talking Chick

Virtual Pet · Free

Talk, feed, dress up and dance with Peep, your cute little talking baby chick. Bath time, disco parties, trips to space and snowy adventures await!

Privacy policy

How to play Peep

  1. Talk to Peep and hear it repeat you in a funny little voice.
  2. Tap the buttons to feed, bathe, dress up and play.
  3. Visit colorful places and dance together at the disco.

Features

  • Peep repeats what you say in a funny little voice
  • Dress-up, bath time, disco dancing and more
  • Visit colorful places, from space to snowland
  • Works offline

Frequently asked questions

Is Peep free?

Yes. Peep is free to download and play on Google Play.

Can I play Peep offline?

Yes. Peep works fully offline, so you can play with Peep anywhere without an internet connection.

Why does Peep need the microphone?

Peep listens so it can repeat what you say in its funny chick voice. The sound is processed on your device only: your voice is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and you can turn the microphone off at any time.

Is Peep on iPhone?

Peep is on Android today. iPhone and iPad versions are planned.
