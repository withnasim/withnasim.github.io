    Play it, or print it

    Most word search sites make you choose: a fiddly online game, or a printable sheet behind a sign-up form. This one does both from the same word list. Play the puzzle in the Play tab, and when you want paper, the Print tab builds a clean A4 sheet with a matching answer key.

    How to play

    Drag from the first letter to the last — straight lines only, sideways, up and down, or diagonally. On a phone, tap the first letter and then the last one. Found words get crossed off the list; the timer starts on your first move.

    Teacher mode: one word list, a whole class set

    In the Print tab, set the number of copies and the generator builds that many different puzzles from the same words — same list, different layout, so the child in the next seat cannot copy the answers. Each puzzle is numbered (Puzzle 1 of 5) and every one has its own answer key, printed after the puzzles in the same order.

    Large print switches to bigger letters and a tighter grid: good for young children, classroom display copies, and readers who need larger type.

    Good words to use

    • Short and varied — mix 4-letter and 8-letter words. All 3-letter words makes a puzzle that solves itself.
    • Themed sets — 10 to 15 words on one topic reads better than 30 random ones.
    • Watch repeated letters — words that share letters are harder to find and more fun.

    Difficulty, without extra settings

    Two toggles cover it: turning off diagonals makes an easy puzzle for young children, and turning on backwards makes an adult puzzle noticeably harder. Leave diagonals on and backwards off for the middle setting that suits most family puzzles.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No account, no watermark, no limit on how many puzzles you play or print.

    Do you store my words?

    No. The page runs entirely in your browser — once it has loaded it works without a connection, and your words are only held in memory.

    How do I print a different puzzle for each student?

    Open the Print tab and choose the number of copies under Teacher mode. Each copy is a different layout of the same words, with its own answer key.

    Can I use the puzzles commercially?

    The puzzles you build with your own words are yours — classrooms, parties, church bulletins, work newsletters. Please don't resell the tool itself.

    Why are some of my words missing from the printed sheet?

    Words that are too long for the grid, or that repeat a word already placed, are listed under the puzzle. Shorten them or choose a larger grid.
