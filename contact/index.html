Contact Najura

The fastest way to reach us is email: info@najura.com. We answer within two working days.

Reporting a bug

Include the game name, your phone model, your Android version, and what you were doing when it happened. If the game froze or a level could not be solved, tell us the level number and we will look at that first.

Feedback and ideas

Tell us what you would change. We cannot promise to build it, but we read every message and we keep a list. If you send a suggestion, you allow us to use it to improve the games without payment.

Business and press

For partnerships, licensing or press enquiries, put what you have in mind in the first line of the email so it reaches the right person.

Privacy requests

If you want to know what information we hold about you, or want it deleted, write to the same address with "privacy" in the subject line. Our privacy policy explains what we keep.
