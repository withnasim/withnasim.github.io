Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Najura is an independent game studio, a brand of Najuvia LLC. This page explains what information is collected when you visit najura.com, what our games and apps collect, and what choices you have. It covers this website; each game also has its own policy, linked at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

You can read this site without an account and without giving us any personal information. If you email us, we keep your message and your address so we can reply. If you install a game, the game may record anonymous gameplay information such as the level reached, session length and crash reports. That information is used to find bugs and adjust difficulty. It is not linked to your name.

Cookies and advertising

This site and our games may show advertising served by Google AdSense, Google AdMob and their partners. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent notice before advertising cookies are set, and can change that choice at any time.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand which pages are read and which guides help. These tools record the pages visited, approximate location, browser and device type. They do not identify you personally, and we do not combine them with any information that does.

Children

Our games are family friendly and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Peep, our game for younger players, has no accounts, no chat, no external links and no behavioural advertising. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and to object to how it is processed. Write to info@najura.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Data retention and security

Email correspondence is kept for as long as it is useful for support, and deleted on request. Analytics data is retained in aggregate. We use reputable providers, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes are announced on the blog.
