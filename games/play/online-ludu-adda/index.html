Online Ludu Adda

The adda you grew up with, in your browser: ludo with dice and cutting, plus a full sap ludu board — two to four players, no download.

Two games on one page

Online Ludu Adda carries the two boards Bangladeshi families actually play. The home screen asks which one you want before the room opens:

  • Ludu (লুডু) — four tokens each, one dice, star safe squares, cutting, and a home column that needs the exact number to finish.
  • Sap Ludu (সাপলুডু) — snakes and ladders on a 1 to 100 board. Ladders lift you, snakes drop you, and you must land exactly on 100 to win.

Both boards sit in the same room, so the same link, theme and players carry over. When a game ends the board turns into a small cartoon scene: the winning token bounces with a crown, the losing token topples over, and a comic line picks itself from what actually happened in the match.

How to play ludo

  1. Tap the dice. A six brings a token out of its home base, and a six always earns another roll.
  2. Tap a glowing token to move it. Tokens walk one square at a time, with a tick for every square, so you always see where they landed.
  3. Landing on a square with an opponent sends that token back home — unless it is a star square, which is safe.
  4. Stars also mark every colour's starting square, so nobody can be cut on their first step.
  5. After the full lap, a token turns into its coloured home column. Reach the centre with all four tokens to win.

How to play sap ludu

  1. Roll the dice and your piece walks that many squares, from 1 upwards.
  2. Land on the bottom of a ladder and you climb it at once, with a rising chime.
  3. Land on a snake's head and you slide back down to its tail.
  4. You need the exact number to reach 100. Overshooting leaves you where you are, so late rolls can stall a whole turn.
  5. A six gives you another roll in both games.

Themes and a 2D mode

The home screen offers five board themes — classic gold, night, neon, wood and mint — plus a 2D flat skin that removes the shadows and gradients. Whatever you pick is remembered on your device, so the next visit opens on the board you like.

On a phone the board rotates so your own colour always sits at the bottom-left, the way a seated player would see the real board. You can check any other player's view with the eye button above the board.

Tips that win games

  • Keep one token safely out and moving rather than bringing all four out slowly. A token in the base is a token that cannot be cut.
  • In ludo, park on star squares when an opponent's token is one roll behind you.
  • Do not rush the home column with a token that is still far back — the last stretch needs exact numbers.
  • In sap ludu, count the ladders before you roll. A board with a ladder at 74 is worth chasing; a snake at 76 is worth avoiding.
  • Watch the turn timer. Passing two turns in a row usually costs more than a cautious token.

Frequently asked questions

Is Online Ludu Adda free to play?

Yes. Both ludo and sap ludu are free in the browser. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

What is the difference between ludu and sap ludu?

Ludu uses four tokens per player with a dice, safe stars and cutting. Sap ludu is snakes and ladders: one piece per player on a 1 to 100 board, with ladders that lift you and snakes that send you back.

How many people can play?

Two to four. Pick the game, open a room, then start. Empty seats are filled by computer players, so a full board is always possible alone.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. Open the page on a phone, tablet or computer and tap play. The whole game runs in the browser.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The board scales to the screen width, the dice and reaction buttons sit at the bottom for thumbs, and on phones the board stays flat so nothing is cut off.

Can I play with a friend far away?

Yes. Open a room, copy the invite link and send it to up to three friends. Everyone joins the same board from their own phone, and the dice are rolled by the server so nobody can cheat. Empty seats are filled by the computer if you want to play alone.
