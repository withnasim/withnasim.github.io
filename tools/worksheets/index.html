How to make a worksheet in three steps

Choose the type of sheet you need, fill in the few boxes on the left, and press Print / save PDF. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. The word search is generated in your browser with a fixed pattern for each set, so the paper you print today and the copy you print next month are identical.

For a spelling list, paste the words one per line. Two to twelve words fill a 12 × 12 grid nicely; if a word is longer than the grid, it is skipped — that is usually a sign to move up to a 15 × 15 grid. The instruction line at the top of the page still makes sense for every layout, so you can print a different difficulty for different reading groups without rewriting the directions.

Why the sets come in A, B and C

Teachers rarely need one worksheet; they need thirty of the same worksheet that do not copy. Sets A, B and C keep the same words, the same instructions and the same difficulty, but the letters are rearranged. Put set A on the left of the room and set C on the right, and the two children who always trade answers have nothing to trade.

Answer keys that actually match

The key page is generated from the same puzzle as set A. Hidden words are highlighted in the grid, and underneath you get the row and column of the first and last letter, so you can check a finished sheet in seconds instead of hunting for the word yourself. For math drills the key prints the answers in place, which makes it usable for a quick self-check station as well.

Printing tips

  • Print at 100% scale, not “fit to page”, so the grid stays square and the letters stay legible.
  • Grayscale is fine — the paper sheets are already black on white, and the highlighted key still reads when printed in mono.
  • Choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog to keep a copy on the class drive or to send the sheet home.
  • Print the answer key separately if you are handing the worksheet out in class.

What this tool does not do

It does not read a curriculum, grade a student or store anything about them. Nothing you type leaves your computer — the page has no accounts, no upload and no tracking of the words you enter. That is also why it keeps working on a school laptop with no internet, once the page has loaded.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes — every type, every grid size, all answer keys, no watermark, no daily limit. There is nothing to buy later.

Can I use the sheets in my classroom?

Print them for your own students, your own children or your tutoring sessions as often as you like. Please do not resell the generated files as your own product.

Does it work on a phone?

The generator runs on a phone, but the print dialog is happier on a laptop or desktop. For a class set, build the sheet on a computer and print from there.

Why is one of my words missing?

A word longer than the grid cannot be placed. Shorten it, remove the spaces, or pick a larger grid — the sheet footer tells you which grid size was used.
