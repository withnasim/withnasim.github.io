Why these tools are free

Najura is a small independent studio. We make cozy puzzle games for phones, and we build these browser tools for the same reason: they are the kind of thing we wanted ourselves and could not find without a sign-up wall. The worksheet generator came out of a simple problem — making thirty different copies of one worksheet by hand takes an hour that a teacher would rather spend on the lesson.

There is no advertising inside the tools and no account to create. The pages are plain HTML and JavaScript, so they load fast on a school laptop and keep working after the Wi-Fi drops. Nothing you type into them is sent anywhere.

What is coming next

More classroom sheets in the same style — reading comprehension, fractions, and a set of ready-to-print topic packs for early grades. If there is a specific worksheet you keep rebuilding by hand every term, write to us at info@najura.com and it may be the next one we build.
