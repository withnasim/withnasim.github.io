About Najura

Najura is a small independent studio that makes free, offline puzzle games and a few friendly apps. Brickwise is a block puzzle. Potion Pour is a colour sort puzzle. Gilded is a memory card game. Peep is a talking pet for younger players. Everything is made by a small team, without an advertising budget, and without interrupting play with forced videos.

Why puzzles

A puzzle is the only kind of game that respects a five minute break. You open it, you play one round, you close it and go back to your day. We design for that break rather than for a session that never ends. That is why there are no energy timers, no streaks that punish you for missing a day, and no paywalls in the middle of a level.

How we build

Every game starts on paper. We draw the board, play it with a pencil, and throw away the versions that are only fun once. The build that survives is the one that runs smoothly on a cheap Android phone, because that is the phone most of our players actually own. We test with family, neighbours and friends, and we write down what they do wrong, because a tutorial that has to explain the rules has already failed.

How we make money

The games are free and stay free. They show advertising, and that is the whole business model. We do not require accounts, we do not sell data, and we do not run reward schemes that pay people to install something. This website also carries advertising; the guides on it are written by us and exist to help players, not to fill space.

Where we are

Najura is a brand of Najuvia LLC. We work remotely, and our players are mostly in Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Indonesia and the Philippines, with a growing number in the United States and the United Kingdom.

The name

We started out making puzzle games under the name Naj Studio. As we began planning apps as well as games, we wanted one name that could cover everything we make, so the studio became Najura. Games, guides, tools and everything else now live on this one site — nothing you had saved, installed or bookmarked stops working.
